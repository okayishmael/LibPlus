Launch Strategy

90-Day Launch Plan

Three decisive phases from groundwork to full public launch, designed for a lean team powered by AI.

01
Days 1–30 · Foundation

Build the Machine

  • Register LiberiaVoice brand, domain, and LLC/NGO entity
  • Build responsive website (WordPress/Webflow + custom design)
  • Set up all 5 social media accounts with consistent branding
  • Draft 20 evergreen articles across all 11 sectors with Claude AI
  • Create WhatsApp broadcast list and first 500 subscriber push
  • Record pilot podcast episode (editor intro + top 3 stories)
  • Build pre-launch email list via waitlist landing page
  • Set up Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, and SEO baseline
  • Recruit 5–10 diaspora contributors (no pay — prestige + byline)
  • Establish Claude AI content workflow and editorial calendar
02
Days 31–60 · Soft Launch

Go Live, Build Noise

  • Publish first 10 articles — 1 per sector — with social amplification
  • Go live on Facebook with a Liberia-focused launch event stream
  • Launch WhatsApp daily digest to initial subscriber base
  • Partner with 3 Liberian influencers for launch amplification
  • Pitch LiberiaVoice to diaspora Facebook groups in US, UK, Germany
  • Launch first TikTok video series: "Liberia in 60 Seconds"
  • Begin outreach to 5 potential advertising clients (banks, telecoms)
  • Host first Twitter/X Space: "State of Liberia's Economy"
  • Hit 1,000 social followers total across all platforms
  • Collect 500 email subscribers — launch free newsletter
03
Days 61–90 · Full Launch

Scale & Monetize

  • Full public launch with press release to Liberian media networks
  • Launch premium subscription tier at $5/month
  • Publish first LiberiaVoice investigative special report
  • Run first paid social media campaign targeting diaspora in US & UK
  • Sign first advertising contract with a Liberian or regional brand
  • Launch YouTube channel with first 5 video articles
  • Host virtual summit: "Liberia's Future Forum" (ticketed, $10–20)
  • Reach 5,000 total social followers across platforms
  • Hit 1,000+ email subscribers and 100 premium subscribers
  • Publish Month 3 analytics report and refine content strategy
