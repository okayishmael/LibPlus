Ask readers to pay

AI tools give anyone free access to news, summaries, and analysis on demand. Liberians with limited spending power will not pay $5/month for content they can get free. Chasing subscriptions in this market is a losing strategy from day one.

Sell access to your audience — to those who can pay.

NGOs, telecoms, governments, diaspora businesses, and development agencies all hold real budgets. They cannot reach your specific, educated, English-literate Liberian audience anywhere else. You are the bridge — and the bridge charges a toll.

01

Highest income potential

Money comes from outsiders, not readers — organizations with hard currency budgets who need your audience

NGO & Development Agency Ads Highest $$$
INGOs, UN agencies, and development orgs pay premium USD rates to reach educated Liberians. USAID, UNICEF, WHO, IRC, NED — they all have marketing budgets and need your audience.
Per deal: $500 – $3,000
Diaspora Business Advertising Highest $$$
Liberian businesses in the US, UK, and Europe need to reach the homeland. Remittance services, shipping companies, law firms, real estate — they can't find your audience anywhere else.
Per deal: $300 – $1,500 / month
Telecom & Mobile Money High $$$
Lonestar Cell MTN, Orange Liberia, and mobile money platforms have the largest advertising budgets in Liberia. One partnership covers months of operating costs.
Per deal: $1,000 – $5,000 / month
Government Public Notices Reliable $$$
Ministries are legally required to publish public notices and tenders. They pay per insertion. They use outdated print — you offer digital reach at lower cost and higher impact.
Per notice: $200 – $800

    02

    Earned media & service fees

    You sell skills and platform, not just eyeballs — recurring retainer income with high margins

    Content Agency Services Strong potential
    Use your AI-powered editorial team to produce content for Liberian businesses, NGOs, and politicians. Social media management, press releases, annual reports, grant writing.
    Retainer: $300 – $1,200 / month per client
    Sponsored Events & Summits Strong potential
    Virtual summits sponsored by NGOs or embassies. Ticket revenue from diaspora. Physical events in Monrovia sponsored by corporates. One good event equals months of ad revenue.
    Per event: $2,000 – $15,000
    Jobs & Tenders Board Passive income
    NGOs, embassies, and companies pay to list jobs and tenders. Liberians trust listings from credible media. Charge per listing or monthly — near-zero marginal cost once built.
    Per listing: $20 – $150 · 30+ listings / month possible
    Liberian Business Directory Passive income
    The definitive, trusted directory of Liberian businesses worldwide — home and diaspora. Restaurants in Atlanta, lawyers in London, doctors in Minnesota. Recurring monthly listing revenue.
    Per listing: $5 – $50 / month · scalable to 500+

      03

      Digital products & affiliate revenue

      Zero audience spending power required — you earn from third-party companies, not your readers

      Affiliate & Referral Programs Grows over time
      Earn commissions from remittance services (Wise, Sendwave, WorldRemit) every time a reader sends money home via your link. Also VPN services, hosting, and online courses.
      Per referral: $5 – $25 · fully passive
      Journalism Grants & Press Funds Underused goldmine
      Google News Initiative, Meta Journalism Project, INN, and Press Freedom orgs fund African digital media. Many grants go unclaimed. One grant funds 12 months of operations.
      Per grant: $5,000 – $50,000
      WhatsApp Channel Sponsorship Fast to monetize
      Once you have 5,000+ subscribers, charge a brand to co-sponsor the daily digest. "Today's news brought to you by X." You write the digest anyway — add one line and get paid.
      Monthly: $200 – $800 per sponsor
      YouTube AdSense Builds over time
      YouTube pays automatically once you qualify. Diaspora viewers in the US and UK earn 10–20x more per view than Africa-based traffic. Diaspora-targeted content is financially superior.
      At 10K subs: $200 – $800 / month passively
